ItsyBIT EVE Template

Event modeling · Vertical slices · Event sourcing

A starting point for building an app with your AI assistant. You and the AI agree on a plan in an Event Book first, slice by slice. Then the AI builds it test by test, and you watch every scenario turn green.

How it goes

  1. Make your own copy of this repo and open your AI assistant in it. Say let's plan a todo app, or whatever you want to build.
  2. The AI writes the plan and opens the Event Book: every thing your app can do, with Given / When / Then examples. Comment on anything, add the examples you're missing, tick Refined when a slice is right, then press Done with feedback.
  3. Tell the AI done. It answers every comment and shows the next round. Repeat until every slice is refined.
  4. Say start building. The AI builds one slice at a time, tests first. When it's finished, open the app in your browser.
  5. Say make it beautiful. The AI turns your plan into a design brief for your design tool (Claude Design, Figma, v0, whatever you use), then restyles the app from what comes back. Every test still has to pass.

Start

On GitHub, press Use this template to make your own copy (it can be private). Then:

git clone https://github.com/you/my-app
cd my-app
claude            # or Cursor, Codex, any assistant that reads AGENTS.md
> let's plan a todo app

You need Node 18 or newer; there's nothing to install. When EVE gets better, your Event Book says so: tell your AI update EVE. It never touches your plan or your app.
